Abstract:
    The Binarity at LOw Metallicity (BLOeM) survey is an ESO large
    programme designed to obtain multi-epoch spectroscopy for 929 massive
    stars in the Small Magellanic Cloud (SMC). It will provide binary
    fractions and orbital configurations of binary systems, and search for
    dormant black-hole binary candidates (OB+BH). Here we present
    projected rotational velocities (vsini) of all sources and, using the
    multiplicity properties presented in previous papers, we derive the
    vsini distributions of apparent single stars, single lined
    spectroscopic (SB1) binaries, and SB2 systems. We identify a locus in
    the Hertzsprung-Russell diagram where rotational velocities decrease
    significantly; we interpret this feature as broadly corresponding to
    the terminal-age main sequence. The main sequence cohort is
    distinguished by a broad range of vsini values, but with a strong peak
    in the distribution in the range 30-60km/s, close to the resolution
    limit of ~30km/s. Sources in this low vsini peak are distributed
    throughout the main sequence, and are also present in the SB1 sample,
    though less prominent than in the single star distribution. A
    preliminary analysis of the lowest vsini cohort, that includes SB1
    systems, implies that roughly one third may be nitrogen rich and we
    speculate that this cohort is a mix of pristine single stars, long
    period binaries, and merger products. The SB2 systems appear to be
    mostly short period binaries in synchronous rotation and have vsini
    estimates distributed around a mean value of ~140km/s. Higher vsini
    sources are also present in the single and SB1 systems, all of which
    have tail to higher vsini values, consistent with tidal and
    mass-transfer effects. The supergiants, with a few exceptions, have
    low vsini, the bulk of these systems being essentially unresolved at
    current spectral resolution (~30km/s).

Description:
    We present projected rotational velocities for 929 sources from the
    multi-epoch Binarity at LOw Metallicity (BLOeM) spectroscopic survey
    of the Small Magellanic Cloud using VLT/Flames.

    Table 1: Projected rotational velocities of all BLOeM stars from
    Shenar et al. (2024A&A...690A.289S, Cat. J/A+A/690/A289) obtained
    using the Fourier transform (FT) and full-width half-maximum (FWHM)
    methods. Projected rotational velocities for the 70 SB2 systems listed
    in Table 2 should be preferred to the values in this table.

    Table 2: Projected rotational velocities for both components,
    labeled A and B, of 70 SB2 systems obtained using the HeI 4387{AA}
    line. These were determined using the FWHM approach (Gaussian
    fitting). To facilitate identification of A and B, the central
    wavelengths of each HeI 4387 A component, waveA and waveB, are
    also listed for a specific observation with epoch number nepoch.

    Table C.1: Master table listing additional auxiliary information
    for convenience. Stellar parameters and binary status are from
    Bestenlehner et al (2025MNRAS.540.3523B, Cat. J/MNRAS/540/3523),
    Shenar et al. (2024A&A...690A.289S, Cat. J/A+A/690/A289),
    Sana et al. (2025NatAs...9.1337S, Cat.J/other/NatAs/9.1337),
    Bodensteiner et al. (2025A&A...698A..38B, Cat. J/A+A/698/A38),
    Villasenor et al. (2025A&A...698A..41V, Cat. J/A+A/698/A41),
    Britavskiy et al. (2025A&A...698A..40B, Cat. J/A+A/698/A40)
    and Patrick et al. (2025A&A...698A..39P, Cat. J/A+A/698/A39).
    In additional a number of data flags denoted demographic groups
    as follows:
    field: Field number for that source
    pflag: source of stellar parameters
           = -1 no parameters available
           =  0 parameters from Shenar et al. (2024A&A...690A.289S)
           =  1 parameters from Bestenlehner at al. (2025MNRAS.540.3523B)
           =  2 parameters from Patrick et al. (2025A&A...698A..39P)
    mflag: multiplicity flag
           = -1 not set
           =  0 assumed single
           =  1 not used
           =  2 SB1
           =  3 SB2
    gflag: group flag
           =  0 O-stars
           =  1 B-type dwarfs and giants
           =  2 B-type supergiants and bright giants
           =  3 OBe star
           =  4 BAF supergiants
